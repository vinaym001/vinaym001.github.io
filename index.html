About

Turning Compliance Chaos
Into Clean Systems

I'm a Software Engineer with 3+ years of experience building production-grade web applications. I specialize in statutory compliance integration — designing systems that connect with Indian government portals to automate data sync for chartered accountant and company secretary firms.

I build systems that sync data and documents from 6+ government portals (Income Tax, GST, TAN, TRACES, GSTAT, ICAI), processing notices, orders, e-proceedings, demands, returns, and filing statuses.

My day-to-day spans the full stack: NestJS and TypeORM on the backend, React.js and Material UI on the frontend, with MySQL, MongoDB, and multi-cloud storage integrations across AWS S3, Microsoft OneDrive, and Google Drive.

Outside work, I'm expanding into AI integration through personal projects, including a RAG assistant that answers questions from compliance data.
